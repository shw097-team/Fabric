2. **A signing implementation is not proof of compliant consumption.**  
   `owner_window.py` contains dedicated release/sourcefreeze support, but that cannot establish original Publisher qualification. Its implementation and older blanket-prohibition prose must be reconciled before relying on it.

3. **A signed grant is not evidence that its writeset was enforced.**  
   `issue_grant.py` binds writeset, epoch, subject, candidate, nonce and authorization digest. Its inspected `verify()` checks cryptographic/ledger/expiry conditions; the actual effect consumer must separately enforce scope and preimages. The script also hardcodes a different workspace root. I did not inspect keys or claim a usable current grant.

4. **Containers provide an environment, not original authority.**  
   They can satisfy portable deterministic experiments with bound inputs. They do not automatically satisfy Windows Token/ACL, original Host, protected Checker or Publisher requirements.
