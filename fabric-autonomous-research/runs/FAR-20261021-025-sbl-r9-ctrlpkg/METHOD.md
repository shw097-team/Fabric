# METHOD — FAR-20261021-024 (SBL R9, control-package alignment)

## Question
Under the current Fabric governance contract, with HG-KSEOS as the sole control plane, what is
the best engineering ruling on four things: (a) the residual/gap/blocker/unclosed set, (b) a
complete on-host SBL installation across HERMES/FABRIC/HGK/CODEX/CODEX-CLI, (c) integrating
PLAN+Commander-SP and VERIFY+Acceptance-Challenge-SP, (d) executing SBL_PIPD-LS-PKG DOC-00..13
in full — with that package as the ONLY engineering contract.

## Method
1. Four independent read-only workers, one per question, served by gpt-6.1-sol via
   openai-codex at rank 1 (306-581 s each). Read-only so that research could not quietly
   become an unadmitted write.
2. Each worker told to quote evidence verbatim rather than paraphrase, and to name what it
   could not open rather than infer it.
3. Cross-worker convergence treated as the interesting signal, not any single worker's list.
4. Ruling written before execution, so the execution could not retro-fit the ruling.
5. Execution limited to items whose close condition is falsifiable on this host without an
   owner decision or an external artefact. Everything else named and blocked with a typed
   reason rather than attempted and reported as partial progress.
6. Every executed claim re-measured after the fact and written into a self-check whose
   checks include two that exist only to catch this round's own failure mode.

## Anti-method (explicitly not done)
- No worker was asked whether the project was on track; the question was what is true.
- No percentage of contract completion was computed, because the contract says the
  denominator is unknown and a number would have been our own counting choice.
- No gate was cited for a property it does not directly measure. This is the round's
  central discipline and it is the direct result of the finding below.
