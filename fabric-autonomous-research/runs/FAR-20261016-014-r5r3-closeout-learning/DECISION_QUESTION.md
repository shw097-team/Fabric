# Decision question

Round R5R3-20261016-preview4-closeout asked: for every residual, gap, blocked item, owner-decision item and
candidate-not-landed item of the preview.4 release — and for the prior successful private-plugin upload
procedure — what is the best engineering action set that is low-friction, high-feasibility, of high
engineering necessity, not over-engineered, and aligned with the governing documents?

Sub-questions the round had to answer with measurement, not opinion:
1. Can the released plugin ZIP be proven to be the deterministic product of its recorded source commit?
2. Which causes of the 2026-10-14 host refusal survive measurement, and which are excluded?
3. Which open tickets are actually still open (versus repaired outside the round, or misattributed)?
4. Which items are owner-gated, and is each one action away?
